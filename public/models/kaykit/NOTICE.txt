Spine Vision removes animation clips and their unused binary buffers to reduce
file size. Geometry, skinning, textures, and original character clothing remain.
At runtime we hide optional weapons and hand props, lower the arms, raise the
heads toward the sun, and vary scale. The two front figures use distinct hair
and clothing colors through a material shader; original textures are unchanged.
Mouse tracking keeps each body fixed and only moves the head within a natural
rotation range. Targets beyond that range, and pointer exit, restore the sun gaze.
The sun and its halo float together within the visible canvas bounds.
The sun, lighting, ground, and shadows are composed in Three.js.
